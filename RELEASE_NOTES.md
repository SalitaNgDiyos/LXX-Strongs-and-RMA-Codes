Tag fixes in the three LXX modules. Each file below was updated in this release; put the `.ot` files in your theWord `Modules` folder.

- **Perfect participles retagged.** 27 words in Brenton+, 26 in SweteLXX+ and 29 in Rahlfs+ were tagged as aorist or present but are perfect participles (e.g. ἠφανισμένην, ἠλειμμένοι, ἐξωσμένον, ἐπῃρμένοι, τεθλασμένον). They now have perfect morphology (V-RMP / V-RPP, or XMP / XPP in Rahlfs+).
- **διαρπάζω (G1283).** All verb forms (31 to 32 per module) had the noun διαρπαγή as their lemma; the lemma is now διαρπάζω.
- Earlier in this update series: Exod 12:9 ὠμὸν → G13149 ὠμός and ἡψημένον → G5952 ἕψω; Josh 9:4 ὤμων → G5606 (release 2026-11-18_0848).

Use with the Greek Bible Lexicon dictionary (https://github.com/SalitaNgDiyos/Greek-Bible-Lexicon).
