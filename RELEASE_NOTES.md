Tag fixes in the three LXX modules (LXX Brenton+, SweteLXX+, LXX Rahlfs+):

- Exod 12:9 ὠμὸν "raw" now has the new number G13149 ὠμός (it was tagged G5606 ὦμος "shoulder")
- Exod 12:9 ἡψημένον "boiled" now has ἕψω G5952 with perfect participle morphology (it was tagged G1530 εἰσπηδάω in Brenton+ and SweteLXX+; Rahlfs+ had the wrong tense)
- Josh 9:4 ὤμων "shoulders" now has G5606 (Brenton+ and SweteLXX+ had G3688 ὄνος "donkey")

Unzip and put the `.ot` files in your theWord `Modules` folder. Use with the Greek Bible Lexicon dictionary (https://github.com/SalitaNgDiyos/Greek-Bible-Lexicon), which has the new G13149 entry.
